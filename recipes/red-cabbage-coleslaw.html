Red Cabbage Slaw

Ingredients

Dressing
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1 tbl spoon ginger paste
  • 1 tbl spoon low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbl spoons garlic paste
  • 1/4 tea spoon chilli paste
  • 1 tea spoon sesame seeds
Salad
  • 1/2 red cabbage, thinly sliced
  • 1 English Cucumber, sliced
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 small bunch cilantro, chopped

Steps

  1. Add olive oil, toasted sesame oil, rice vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, ginger paste, garlic paste, salt, chili paste, and sesame seeds to a jar with a lid. Whisk together and then cover. Store in the refrigerator until ready for use.

  2. Place cabbage, cucumber, green onions, and cilantro in a bowl. Toss and then drizzle dressing over by teaspoons. Toss and taste before adding more dressing.
